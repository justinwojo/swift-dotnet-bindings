## Grok review and adjudication

Round 1 reviewed the complete historical range and the integrated Stage B index.
It reported two High, two Medium, and two Low findings. All were accepted:

- Canonical evidence-path comparison did not resolve ancestor symlinks.
- Method-generic optional-class returns retained the optional container directly.
- `evidence_directory` was not request-file-relative.
- SurfaceAccounting receipt recording lacked explicit post-Validate ordering.
- Nested command/stage receipt objects were underspecified in the JSON schema.
- A CSM recursion comment was stale.

Round 2 resumed the same Grok session, verified all six fixes, and completed the
remaining historical file groups. It reported no Critical, High, or Medium issue.
Its single new Low finding was accepted: synthesized property and subscript
getter/setter methods did not preserve `RawGenericSig`, hiding lossless-only
constraints such as `BitwiseCopyable` from the existing marker gate. All four
accessor constructors now preserve the raw signature, with a parser regression
covering the four paths.

The final Low repair was not sent through a third review. That is intentional:
the paired-review stopping rule ends after one worthwhile Low-only repair pass,
and the user required re-review only after High/Critical repairs.
