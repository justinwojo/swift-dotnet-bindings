## OWNER-02 resolution

The owner selected the standing prediction-freeze policy rather than an exception.
The exact Foundation `NSInvocation` member pre-skip, the synchronous CSM internal-
conformer admission check, and the lossless marker/concrete-pin expansion of the
generic-parent member predictor were removed. The shared wrapper post-processor no
longer strips internal-type or `NSInvocation` blocks either; their manifest buckets
remain zero-valued for schema compatibility.

Those shapes now follow one consumer contract: render the candidate and its origin
metadata, compile it, attribute swiftc diagnostics to the owning member/accessor
leaf, add only that recovery unit to the denylist, and render again. Recorded Xcode
26.3 diagnostics cover all three removed families and prove two-round convergence
while a healthy sibling remains outside the denylist. Deterministic placeholder
cleanup remains in `SwiftWrapperPostProcessor`; it is not a compiler-legality model.

Grok final review session `01a09e63-dfde-7243-8c0b-e49458fb4e22`
covered the complete frozen OWNER-02 candidate. It found no Critical, High, or
Medium defect. Its single Low finding identified a stale recovery-note trigger
that still invited plan-stage prediction for compiler-catchable constrained
extensions; the trigger was aligned with the prediction freeze in the final
Low-only repair pass. Per the requested stopping rule, that documentation-only
repair was not re-reviewed.
